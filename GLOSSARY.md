# Emulsion

A desktop app that develops photos through film stock looks. These are the words the code, docs and conversations use for its concepts.

## Retouching

**Retouch spot**:
One edit in a photo's ordered list of retouch edits: a shape, the tool applied inside it, and its opacity. Each spot works on the result of the spots before it.
_Avoid_: Heal spot (Heal is one tool), retouch layer

**Retouch tool**:
What a retouch spot does inside its shape: Heal, Clone, Fill, Blur or Remove.

**Fill**:
The retouch tool that paints a flat colour (or erases to grey) inside the shape, from darktable's retouch module.
_Avoid_: using "fill" for what Remove produces

**Remove**:
The retouch tool that replaces what is under its shape with plausible surroundings generated by an AI inpainting model.
_Avoid_: AI Remove, generative remove, content-aware fill, erase

**Patch**:
The pixels Remove generated for one spot, stored apart from the photo's edits and regenerated when missing.
_Avoid_: fill, inpaint result
