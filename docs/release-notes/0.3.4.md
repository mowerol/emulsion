Filter the Library by camera, lens, focal length, ISO and file type; tidy away imports, folders and collections; black and white RAWs open in black and white; Panasonic lens correction.

- Filter the Library by camera, lens, focal length, ISO and file type. Choose Metadata in the Library bar to open a row of filters under it, beside Keyword. Lenses are grouped by camera, and ISO can be at most, exactly or at least a value. Smart collections gain Lens, Focal length and ISO rules. Photos already in the Library are read again once in the background to learn their lens and settings, the open folder first. (#62, #63)
- Remove an import or a folder from the Library, and delete collections, from the right-click menu or the More menu. Photos stay on disk, and importing them again brings back their edits; deleting a collection leaves its photos alone. (#65)
- RAWs shot in a camera's black and white or sepia mode now open in black and white (B&W Classic, or B&W Dynamic for high-contrast modes) or sepia, for Panasonic, OM System/Olympus, Sony, Ricoh/Pentax, Nikon and Canon. Photos you have edited keep their edit. (#64)
- Panasonic Lumix RAWs (such as the LX3) now have their lens distortion corrected from the profile the camera records in the file. (#61)

Known: Remove can still produce smudges or invented details, especially on difficult backgrounds. The Fuji recipe editor currently supports colour recipes. Recipe detail settings use Emulsion's own grain, clarity and sharpening; a recipe's camera dynamic-range setting, noise reduction and exposure compensation are not applied. Some colours, particularly saturated greens, can still differ from the camera's JPEG. Very heavy exports of GFX100RF photos can still run out of memory.

Please report any problems at github.com/mowerol/emulsion/issues, with the log file attached (Help › Show Log in Finder).
