Smoother photo browsing, adjustable zoom, quieter update notices and fixes for Fujifilm RAWs and Sony lens correction.

- Emulsion now checks for updates while it stays open and when you return after a check is due. Available updates appear as a small dismissible notice; choose View Update to see the release notes and installer. Manual checks remain available. (#59)
- Opening Library or Import no longer scans unrelated folders or triggers their access requests. Import shows your chosen folder directly, and unreadable folders offer a native folder picker to grant access. (#18, #58)
- Adjust scroll and pinch zoom sensitivity from 10% to 200% in Settings › Tools, with a Reset button. The setting applies in Develop and Library Loupe and is remembered between launches. (#19, #57)
- Star filters in Library and Develop now offer less than, at most, equal to, not equal to, at least and greater than comparisons. Empty stars let you filter for unrated photos. (#56)
- Embedded lens correction now works for Sony RAWs whose vignette and geometry tables have different sample counts, including the ZV-1. (#55)
- Fujifilm X and GFX RAWs shot at DR200 or DR400 now receive the missing one or two stops of exposure compensation. Adaptive Blacks opens near-black detail while keeping true black, extends to +200, and gives stronger negative adjustments. (#54)
- Adaptive Shadows now responds evenly across its range, matching Highlights more closely and making smaller adjustments easier to control. (#53)

Known: Remove can still produce smudges or invented details, especially on difficult backgrounds. The Fuji recipe editor currently supports colour recipes. Recipe detail settings use Emulsion's own grain, clarity and sharpening; a recipe's camera dynamic-range setting, noise reduction and exposure compensation are not applied. Some colours, particularly saturated greens, can still differ from the camera's JPEG. Very heavy exports of GFX100RF photos can still run out of memory.

Please report any problems at github.com/mowerol/emulsion/issues, with the log file attached (Help › Show Log in Finder).
