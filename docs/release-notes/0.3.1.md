Fujifilm recipes, developed from RAW: choose a community recipe, build your own and keep editing.

- New Fuji recipe editor: choose a film simulation, adjust its recipe settings and preview the result on your RAW photos. Open it from Looks › Create Fuji Recipe, or create a recipe from a film simulation in the Look browser.
- 84 Fuji X Weekly recipes join the built-in looks, with credits to their authors. Reggie's Portra, Classic Cuban Neg and Leica X can now be opened in the recipe editor too.
- Recreate a favourite recipe or build your own, then save it to your library. Organise your looks and recipes into folders, choose a folder when saving, and move looks between folders from the Look browser.
- Recipes bring their own white balance, red/blue shift, Grain Effect and size, Clarity and Sharpness. You can continue adjusting exposure, colour and detail, or give the photo its own white balance.
- Closer Fujifilm recipe rendering, including stronger white-balance shifts, Classic Negative's tone controls and Astia's Colour Chrome response.
- Updated Classic Chrome, Classic Negative, Velvia, Astia, Provia, Eterna and Pro Neg. Hi looks.
- The website now compares five tested community recipes with the camera's JPEGs: mowerol.github.io/emulsion/comparisons
- Built-in Fujifilm recipes now apply their published Grain Effect and size alongside Clarity and Sharpness. All 84 Fuji X Weekly recipes and the three Community Recipes were checked against their published settings. Grain remains editable with Emulsion's sliders.
- Existing photo edits keep their saved grain, clarity and sharpening. Select a recipe again to apply its updated defaults.
- Add-on downloads try a verified Emulsion mirror when Microsoft's ONNX Runtime download is unavailable. Both sources must match the same pinned checksum. Failed downloads now show curl's detailed error.

Known: the Fuji recipe editor currently supports colour recipes. Recipe detail settings use Emulsion's own grain, clarity and sharpening; they are not an exact reproduction of the camera's processing. A recipe's camera dynamic-range setting, noise reduction and exposure compensation are not applied. Some colours, particularly saturated greens, can still differ from the camera's JPEG. Very heavy exports of GFX100RF photos can still run out of memory.

Please report any problems at github.com/mowerol/emulsion/issues, with the log file attached (Help › Show Log in Finder).
