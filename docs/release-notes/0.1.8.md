Fujifilm film simulation looks, camera framing, and a new Emulsion Standard.

- New Emulsion x Fuji looks: Classic Negative, Classic Chrome, Pro Neg Hi, Pro Neg Std, Provia, Velvia, Astia, Eterna and Eterna Bleach Bypass, plus the black and white simulations: Acros and Monochrome (plain and with the yellow, red and green filters) and Sepia. Each was fitted to the camera's own JPEGs over about 70 photos. See them side by side with the camera: mowerol.github.io/emulsion/comparisons
- Unedited Fujifilm RAWs open with the look of the film simulation they were shot with.
- Fujifilm RAWs open at the camera's framing: the aspect ratio chosen on the camera and the digital tele-converter crop. In the crop tool, As shot returns to it and Full sensor shows the whole image (Shift+F). Thanks to etrigan63 for the GFX100RF details in issue #11.
- Emulsion Standard, the default look for RAWs, has been refitted to match Lightroom's Adobe Color. Photos you have already edited keep the look as it was.
- JPEGs and other ordinary photos open exactly as they are, with no look and no extra sharpening, since their camera or app has already developed them.
- The Look browser groups the built-in looks into folders: Emulsion and Emulsion x Fuji.
- A built-in Minimal workspace with a short set of controls in Develop, Color and Masks tabs. Masks can also be added to custom workspaces.
- Error reports in the log now include the steps that led up to them, which makes problems much easier to track down.

Known: the Fuji looks match each simulation at its standard settings. Photos shot with the camera's Color, Highlight or Shadow adjustments, or at DR200/DR400, will look different from the camera's preview (DR200/DR400 shots open darker).

Please report any problems at github.com/mowerol/emulsion/issues, with the log file attached (Help › Show Log in Finder).
