Fujifilm recipe looks, closer Velvia and Classic Negative, and much faster work on GFX photos.

- New Emulsion x Fuji Recipes looks: Reggie's Portra (Reggie B), Classic Cuban Neg (Osan B) and Leica X (carlcruzsnaps), three of the community's most popular film simulation recipes. Each was made and rigorously tested against about 70 photos the camera developed with the recipe's settings (at DR100, without grain). Thanks to their authors.
- New Reala Ace and Nostalgic Neg looks, and RAWs shot in either simulation open with them.
- Velvia and Classic Negative now match the camera more closely, especially on strongly saturated colours. Photos you have already edited keep the look as it was.
- White balance has a new Camera Auto choice for Fujifilm RAWs: the camera's own Auto white balance, without the fine-tune shift set on the camera. It appears when the photo was shot with a shift or a fixed white balance.
- Fujifilm GFX100RF and other very large RAWs (up to 128 MP): AI Denoise works on them (issue #13), exports with Texture, Dehaze and masks come out right (issue #27), and Clone and Heal take a fraction of a second per stroke instead of several seconds (issue #31).
- Large RAWs open and zoom faster: the full resolution shows first and capture sharpening follows a moment later.
- Stepping quickly through RAWs in Develop no longer builds up memory until macOS closes the app.
- The Minimal workspace's White Balance section has the presets menu and Pick neutral (now with Camera Auto).
- Long look names in the Look browser end in an ellipsis, with the full name when you hover over it.
- Fixed: dragging the Library's scrollbar quickly could stop the interface.
- Fixed: thumbnails could go blank while a photo's Remove patches were being restored.
- The comparisons page now includes the recipes: mowerol.github.io/emulsion/comparisons

Known: the Fuji simulation looks match each simulation at its standard settings, so photos shot with the camera's Color, Highlight or Shadow adjustments, or at DR200/DR400, look different from the camera's preview. Very heavy exports of GFX100RF photos (Texture, Dehaze and several masked adjustments together) can still run out of memory.

Please report any problems at github.com/mowerol/emulsion/issues, with the log file attached (Help › Show Log in Finder).
