Fujifilm recipes, developed from RAW: choose a community recipe, build your own and keep editing.

- New Fuji recipe editor: choose a film simulation, adjust its recipe settings and preview the result on your RAW photos. Open it from Looks › Create Fuji Recipe, or create a recipe from a film simulation in the Look browser.
- 84 Fuji X Weekly recipes join the built-in looks, with credits to their authors. Reggie's Portra, Classic Cuban Neg and Leica X can now be opened in the recipe editor too.
- Recreate a favourite recipe or build your own, then save it to your library. Organise your looks and recipes into folders, choose a folder when saving, and move looks between folders from the Look browser.
- Recipes bring their own white balance, red/blue shift, Clarity and Sharpness. You can continue adjusting exposure, colour and detail, or give the photo its own white balance.
- Closer Fujifilm recipe rendering, including stronger white-balance shifts, Classic Negative's tone controls and Astia's Colour Chrome response.
- Updated Classic Chrome, Classic Negative, Velvia, Astia, Provia, Eterna and Pro Neg. Hi looks.
- The website now compares five tested community recipes with the camera's JPEGs: mowerol.github.io/emulsion/comparisons

Known: the Fuji recipe editor currently supports colour recipes. Grain starts off; a recipe's camera dynamic-range setting, noise reduction and exposure compensation are not applied. Some colours, particularly saturated greens, can still differ from the camera's JPEG. Updated Fuji processing can change the rendering of existing recipe edits. Very heavy exports of GFX100RF photos can still run out of memory.

Please report any problems at github.com/mowerol/emulsion/issues, with the log file attached (Help › Show Log in Finder).
