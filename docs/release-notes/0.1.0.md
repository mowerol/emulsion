The first beta of Emulsion: develop RAW and ordinary photos through film looks, on your Mac.

Library
- Import photos from any folder. Emulsion works on them where they are and never changes the originals.
- Grid and Loupe views, folders, recent imports, and collections, including smart collections.
- Stars, colour labels (Lightroom's five colours), and pick and reject flags, with Lightroom's keys.
- Keywords and IPTC fields. Stars, labels, rejects, keywords and IPTC fields are shared with Lightroom, Bridge and other apps through XMP sidecars.

Develop
- Emulsion Standard, a clean neutral rendering, is the starting point for every new photo. The Emulsion collection adds Neg, Chrome, Gold and two B&W looks, beside the bundled film LUT packs.
- Create your own looks, preview them across test photos, and save copies to explore variations.
- Tone, colour and detail tools ported from darktable and RawTherapee: white balance, exposure and tone, the tone curve, colour grading, noise reduction, sharpening, lens correction, Transform, halation, glow, grain and vignette.
- Masks with brushes and one-click Select sky. Heal, Clone, AI Remove and AI Noise Reduction (for RAW files) run on your Mac; the AI models are an optional download the first time you use them.
- Virtual copies, presets, copy and paste of settings, and undo for every edit.

Export
- JPEG, or TIFF and PNG at 8 or 16 bits, with an embedded sRGB profile.
- Filename templates, export presets and metadata options.

Known limits
- Apple Silicon Macs only (M1 or later).
- This beta isn't signed by an Apple developer account: see the install steps for the one-time Open Anyway.
- RAW files from different cameras can come out at slightly different brightness with the same look. This is being worked on.
- Not yet: RAW highlight recovery, colour spaces other than sRGB on export, AVIF and HEIF.

Please report problems at github.com/mowerol/emulsion/issues, with the log file attached (Help › Show Log in Finder).
