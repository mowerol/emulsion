Preview Looks before applying them.

- Hover over a Look in the quick picker or the list/grid browser to preview it on your photo. Moving away restores the applied Look; click to apply your choice.
- Keyboard focus previews Looks too, and Escape closes the picker and restores the applied Look.
- Look list rows now highlight under the pointer and on keyboard focus. Moving between adjacent rows keeps the photo preview continuous.

Please report any problems at github.com/mowerol/emulsion/issues, with the log file attached (Help › Show Log in Finder).
