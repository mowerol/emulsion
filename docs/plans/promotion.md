# Promotion ideas

## Share personal photography edited in Emulsion

Idea from Oli, 2026-11-13.

Oli often gets questions about the edits when sharing personal photos. Use photos
edited in Emulsion as a natural way to introduce the app: share the photography,
then explain the editing process when people ask.

Example reply: “I edited this in Emulsion, a photo editor I'm building.”

Where useful, show the settings or a before/after to answer the question. Be clear
about being the developer, and follow the community's rules on promotion in
comments as well as posts.
