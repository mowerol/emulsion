Colour Grading

One full-size wheel at a time keeps the pad usable in the existing 340 px panel.

Select Shadows, Mid-tones, Highlights or Global. Hue runs around the wheel; chroma increases from its centre. Luminance sits underneath.

By tone exposes saturation, chroma and brilliance separately. Tonal ranges stay folded until needed.

This mock-up previews layout only. Sliders and tabs can be explored; it does not grade an image.
