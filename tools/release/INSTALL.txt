Emulsion {{VERSION}} beta ({{COMMIT}})

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later).

Installing
1. Open the .dmg and drag Emulsion to Applications.
2. Open Emulsion from Applications. macOS says it can't check the app for malicious
   software, because this beta isn't signed by an Apple developer account. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Emulsion was blocked",
   click Open Anyway and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   You only need to do this once per version.

If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", the download was quarantined
without the Open Anyway option. Run this in Terminal, then open it again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Emulsion.app

Emulsion keeps its catalog in ~/Library/Application Support/app.emulsion.desktop and,
by default, catalog backups in ~/Pictures/Emulsion/Backups. Edits, virtual copies and
edit history are saved in the catalog. Settings > Catalog offers optional portable
.emulsion.json exports and XMP metadata sharing; both are off by default.

Existing edit sidecars migrate automatically and are left untouched. Keep catalog
backups enabled, and keep old sidecars until migration is verified and you have a
fresh catalog backup. Older Emulsion builds cannot open the upgraded catalog.

Updates: Emulsion checks shortly after launch and offers to install a new version
(Emulsion › Check for Updates… asks at any time). Updates only work when Emulsion is in
Applications. After an update, macOS may ask again for access to folders like Pictures.
